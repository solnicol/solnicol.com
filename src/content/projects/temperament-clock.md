Twelve pure 3:2 fifths almost return to the starting pitch class, but not quite. They overshoot seven octaves by the Pythagorean comma.

This sketch turns that gap into something visible and audible: a cycle that appears closed, but structurally fails to close.

Built as a small web audio experiment.
