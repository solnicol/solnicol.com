## Intro

A flat white looks finished.

A heart. A surface. One clean image.

But the surface is only temporarily ordered. Stir it, and the heart breaks into filaments, folds and eddies before diffusion erases them into beige.

That brief middle is the whole problem.

## What it does

This is a small experiment for seeing that middle.

Stir the surface. The heart loses its shape, but the liquid briefly gains structure.

Your eye sees a pattern breaking.

The curve tracks the structure that appears before it disappears.

## Why it exists

A finished coffee is too neat.

It hides the transition underneath mixing. Separation is simple. Uniformity is simple. Noise is busy but formless.

The interesting part is the unstable middle.

This surface isolates that moment and lets you disturb it.

The heart almost survives the stirring.

Almost is the interesting part.
